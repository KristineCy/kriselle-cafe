A LITTLE ABOUT US

Welcome to Kriselle Cafe, where every cup is made to brighten your day.

Inspired by the name Kristine Cyrelle, Kriselle Cafe was created as a cozy space where good coffee and meaningful moments come together in a place that feels a little like home.

From the first sip to the last smile, we want every visit to feel comforting, genuine, and memorable.
